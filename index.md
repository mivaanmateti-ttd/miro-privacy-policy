# Privacy Policy – Miro Browser

**Last Updated: November 9, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Miro Browser.

Miro Browser is a web browsing application designed to provide users with a simple way to access websites through their Android devices.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Miro Browser handles information when you use our application.

## 2. Information We Collect

Miro Browser does not intentionally collect, store, or transmit users' personal information to its own servers.

The application does not provide:
- User account registration.
- Cloud synchronization.
- Browsing history storage.
- Bookmark storage.
- Advertising or advertising tracking.
- Third-party analytics or tracking services.

## 3. Browsing and Website Information

Miro Browser allows users to visit websites of their choice.

When you access a website, your device connects to that website. The website operator may independently receive information such as your IP address, browser information, and information associated with your visit.

Such information is handled according to the privacy policy and practices of the website you visit. Miro Browser does not control how external websites collect or use information.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

Miro Browser does not maintain its own browsing history or bookmark records.

Website cookies, cached content, and other website data may be handled or stored locally by the Android browser engine, depending on the app implementation and settings.

Miro Browser does not maintain a separate cloud database of users' browsing activities.

## 5. Information Sharing

Miro Browser does not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

The application does not use advertising networks or third-party analytics services, based on its current configuration.

Information exchanged directly between your device and websites you visit is subject to those websites' own practices.

## 6. Advertising

Miro Browser does not display advertisements and does not use advertising tracking technologies.

## 7. Data Security

We aim to keep the application simple and minimize unnecessary data handling.

Connections to external websites are handled by the Android browser engine. The security of a connection also depends on the website and whether it supports HTTPS.

Users should exercise caution when submitting personal or sensitive information to external websites.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Miro Browser is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

The application does not intentionally collect personal information from children or other users.

## 9. Third-Party Websites

Miro Browser may provide access to websites operated by third parties.

We are not responsible for the privacy policies, content, or security practices of external websites. Users should review the privacy policies of websites they visit.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features or privacy practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date.
